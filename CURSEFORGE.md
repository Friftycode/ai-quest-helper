# AI Quest Helper

Turn what is on your screen into text you can paste into an AI. Click the note icon, press Ctrl+C, done.

## Copy

**Quest export** - The selected quest with objectives, progress, rewards, your position and the zone level range.

**Questie data** - With Questie installed: who gives and takes the quest, and where to kill, loot or use things.

**Bags and gear** - Your bags sorted by what matters for the quest, and your gear with item level, stats and enchants.

**Dialog and books** - NPC dialog, gossip, books and notes.

## Helpers

**Quest arrow** - Points to the nearest unfinished objective.

**Target button** - Left click targets your quest mobs, right click your group's.

**Ready to turn in** - A list of finished quests and where to hand them in.

**Collapse all** - One button to fold or unfold the whole quest log.

## Extras

**World map** - Zone levels, dungeon and raid entrances and a dungeon search.

**Threat** - Threat % under enemy nameplates.

Type `/qc help` in game for all commands.
